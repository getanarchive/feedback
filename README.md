# Anarchive feedback

Anarchive is a free, private photo and video library for the Mac, in beta.
Download it from [getanarchive.com](https://getanarchive.com).

This is where bugs get reported and ideas get voted on, in the open.

- **Found a bug?** [Open a bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug.yml). Search first. If someone has
  already reported it, add a 👍 and anything new you've noticed.
- **Have an idea?** [Suggest it](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml). To vote for an idea that's already here,
  give it a 👍. We read the counts.
- **Questions, or just chatting?** Use [Discussions](../../discussions).
- **Rather not use GitHub?** In Anarchive, choose Help ▸ Send Feedback…, or email
  feedback@getanarchive.com.

## Please don't post

- Your photos or videos, unless we ask for one, and then only one you're happy to make public.
- Your library, or anything from inside it.
- Personal details: names, places, faces or anything else from your pictures you'd rather keep private.

Anarchive keeps your pictures on your Mac. Please keep them there here too.

## What happens next

Bugs get a label when we can reproduce them, and the release notes say when they're fixed. Ideas get a label
when they're planned, and an honest "not for now" when they aren't. This is a small project, so replies aren't
instant, but everything gets read.
